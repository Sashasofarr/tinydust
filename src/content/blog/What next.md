I was hoping for peace but didn't expect it to arrive so quietly and suddenly.

No new messages on the phone. No urgent matters that require my attention. No interesting videos on Youtube. Not a sound nor a drop of cortisol.

To be honest, I'm not used to this - my brain is missing the high speed pursuit after the past few weeks.

From this place of nothingness, this do-over, I must ask myself: What is it that I want? What do I want to make come true?

I'm on Day 5 of the personal training course, and I'm already imagining what it would be like to work with clients. Maybe a few clients at the gym and summer group workouts in the park? Or a way of connecting the physical training with coaching for the mind? How about documenting my journey of tech-fitness transition?

So many possibilities.

There's a Chinese proverb that goes "谋事在人，成事在天“ which translates to you put in the effort of doing the thing and leave the results to the heavens. Sounds like something I should give a try now.
